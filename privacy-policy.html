Privacy Policy

At HarvestTable Kitchen, we respect your right to privacy as deeply as we respect good food. Learn how we handle your personal data.

Home Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 1, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

HarvestTable Kitchen ("we", "our", or "us") operates the culinary website and recipe platform at HarvestTable Kitchen. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect from visitors, newsletter subscribers, and home cooks, and describes how that information is utilized, stored, and protected in based on the site's current information practices.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in a few limited and transparent ways:

  • Newsletter Subscriptions: When you subscribe to "The Wholesome Table", we collect the information needed to provide the specific service you request.
  • Contact Form Inquiries: When you submit a recipe inquiry, substitution question, or collaboration message, we collect your full name, email address, topic, and message content.
  • Home Cook Reviews & Comments: Any feedback, ratings, or user-submitted cooking modifications you voluntarily post on our recipe cards.
  • Non-Personal Browsing Data: Standard server logs, device types, browser versions, and anonymous page interaction metrics collected via privacy-friendly cookies to optimize recipe layout and search speed.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for legitimate culinary and operational purposes:

  • To email seasonal recipe collections, meal plans, and cooking tips (you may unsubscribe at any time with one click).
  • To respond promptly to recipe troubleshooting questions and test kitchen feedback.
  • To monitor recipe popularity and curate relevant categories like 15-minute bowls or gluten-free dinners.
  • To maintain website performance, security, and prevent spam submissions.

4. User-Submitted Photos & Comments

When you submit recipe comments, reviews, or social media photos tagged with #HarvestTable Kitchen, you grant us permission to feature your culinary creation on our community gallery. We will always credit your name or handle and never sell your photographs.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

The site may use browser storage or cookies for functions such as saved preferences, depending on the features enabled. Any analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking tools actually used on the site should be described here accurately.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with a limited number of vetted service providers who adhere to strict data security standards:

  • Email Delivery Services: Secure platforms used exclusively to distribute requested newsletters and downloadable e-books.
  • Hosting & CDN Infrastructure: Cloud hosting providers that ensure fast loading of high-resolution food photography and recipe data.

7. Data Security & Retention

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information submitted through the website. The actual security and retention practices should be kept up to date with the services used by the site. We retain email addresses only as long as you maintain an active subscription to our recipe newsletter.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and your location, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including:

  • Request a copy of any personal information we retain about you.
  • Request immediate correction of inaccurate contact details.
  • Request complete deletion of your data from our mailing lists.
  • Opt-out of any marketing emails instantly using the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of every message.

9. Children's Online Privacy

HarvestTable Kitchen does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.
