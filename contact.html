Kitchen Guidance

Frequently Asked Cooking Questions

Quick solutions to the most common home cook questions regarding substitutions and measurement conversions.

Can I substitute ingredients for allergies?

Yes! All our recipes include direct substitution notes. For instance, tamari can seamlessly replace soy sauce for gluten-free diets, and coconut aminos or maple syrup work for refined-sugar-free dishes.

Are recipe measurements in cups or grams?

Our recipe cards provide both standard US Volume (cups, tablespoons) and precise Metric Weight (grams, milliliters) to ensure absolute precision, especially for artisanal baking.

Can I share your recipes on my blog?

You are welcome to write about your experience cooking our recipes! We kindly ask that you rewrite the instructions in your own words and provide a clickable credit link back to the original HarvestTable Kitchen recipe page.
